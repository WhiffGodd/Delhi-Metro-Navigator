💳 Smart Card Recharge

Recharge your DMRC Metro Smart Card online directly on the official DMRC portal.

ℹ️ Directs to official DMRC portal (dmrcsmartcard.com). After payment, tap your card at AVM (Add Value Machine) at any station to complete top-up.

ℹ️ Delhi Metro Guide

Delhi Metro is India's largest rapid transit system, operating across Delhi NCR with high frequency and accessibility features.

  • First Train: ~5:30 AM (Mon–Sat) / ~6:00 AM (Sun)
  • Last Train: ~11:30 PM (Terminal departures)
  • Token & Smart Card: Minimum fare ₹10, Maximum ₹60
  • Peak Headways: 2 to 4 mins on major lines
